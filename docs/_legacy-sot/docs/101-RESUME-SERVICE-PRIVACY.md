# Resume Service Privacy

Resume data is processed only to deliver the requested human editing service.
Access is limited to the member, assigned/authorized operational staff, and
audited Super Admin interventions.

Source files, supplements, working files, and deliverables are private and
deleted 90 days after the latest official release. Metadata and sanitized audit
history may remain under the platform retention policy. No document content is
sent to third-party AI in MVP, attached to email, indexed, or captured by
analytics.
